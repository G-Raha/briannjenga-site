About Brian

Over a decade crafting data‑driven content and search strategies that move the needle. My work bridges strategy and storytelling — from SEO content and analytics setups to thought‑leadership ghostwriting trusted by clients across North America, Europe, Australia, and Africa.

I’m also building The Mekatilili Series — Echoes of Valor, Daughter of War, and Curtain Falls — inspired by Mekatilili wa Menza, a courageous Chonyi heroine and early Kenyan resistance icon. As a kinsman and admirer of strong feminist narratives, I believe her story deserves global limelight.
