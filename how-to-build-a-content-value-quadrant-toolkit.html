How to Build a Content Value Quadrant

A 2×2 matrix that helps you evaluate content by both Reach (traffic, shares, impressions) and Depth (saves, replies, conversions).

What’s inside

Step-by-step Quadrant Build: Plot a simple 2×2 grid with Reach vs Depth and label each quadrant:

  1. Quiet Gold (High Depth, Low Reach) – valuable but under-distributed
  2. Loud & Light (High Reach, Low Depth) – awareness drivers
  3. Compounding Assets (High Reach, High Depth) – your MVPs
  4. Shallow Fade (Low Reach, Low Depth) – needs rework or retirement

Scoring system: Use analytics + qualitative signals (shares, mentions, saves) to rate each asset 1–5 on Reach and Depth.

Action playbook: Practical next steps depending on quadrant—boost Quiet Gold with SEO & repurposing, enrich Loud & Light with CTAs, expand Compounding Assets into pillar formats, or cut Shallow Fades.

Final mindset shift: Don’t just chase clicks—invest in trust, resonance, and business value.

Content Value Quadrant — FAQs

What is a Content Value Quadrant?

The Content Value Quadrant is a 2×2 matrix that scores each asset on two axes—Reach and Depth—to reveal four buckets: Quiet Gold, Loud & Light, Compounding Assets, and Shallow Fade. It helps you prioritize optimization, repurposing, and retirement decisions.

How do I score Reach vs. Depth?

Use a 1–5 scale for each axis. Reach = traffic, impressions, shares. Depth = saves, replies, demo sign-ups, purchases, or other conversion events. Blend analytics with qualitative signals (comments, mentions) for a balanced view.

What should I do with “Quiet Gold” content?

Quiet Gold has high Depth but low Reach. Improve internal linking, add SEO metadata, create derivative formats (threads, short video, carousels), and pitch it into newsletters or partner roundups to increase distribution.

How do I turn “Loud & Light” assets into conversions?

Add trust and clarity: stronger CTAs, proof blocks (testimonials, data), related resources, and clearer next steps. Guide readers to deeper, high-intent pages or lead magnets.

When should I retire “Shallow Fade” content?

Retire or consolidate when an asset stays low Reach/low Depth across two review cycles, duplicates other pages, or targets keywords with no strategic value. 301 to a stronger, related page when possible.

How often should I re-score my library?

Quarterly is ideal for most teams. High-velocity sites can review monthly; slower sites can review bi-annually. Re-score after major launches, site migrations, or algorithm updates.

Does this work for B2B and B2C?

Yes. The framework is channel-agnostic and works for blogs, product pages, YouTube, newsletters, and social content in both B2B and B2C contexts.

What tools do I need?

Any web analytics platform (GA4 or similar), search data (GSC), and basic qualitative inputs (CRM notes, support tickets, sales feedback). A spreadsheet is enough to start.
