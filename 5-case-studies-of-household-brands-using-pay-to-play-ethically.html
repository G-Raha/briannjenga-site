🌟 Mini Case Study Pack: Brands Using Paid Visibility Ethically

Not all ads are manipulative. These five case studies show how trust-first, values-driven paid campaigns win both loyalty 💙 and results 📈.

What’s inside

🛒 Aldi — “Shop ALDI First” (Australia)

  • Challenger supermarket turns cost-of-living into trust equity.
  • ✨ Impact: Most trusted supermarket brand + Grand Effie Award.

🌲 Patagonia — “Don’t Buy This Jacket”

  • A radical Black Friday ad that told people not to consume.
  • ✨ Impact: +30% sales boost + cemented as a sustainability leader.

👓 Warby Parker — Buy a Pair, Give a Pair

  • Affordable eyewear with social good baked in.
  • ✨ Impact: 13M+ pairs donated + transparent impact reports.

🌱 Allbirds — Carbon Footprints in Every Ad

  • Eco-footwear brand made emissions labeling mainstream.
  • ✨ Impact: Became a Shopify top-performer + skyrocketed trust.

💎 Dove — “Real Beauty” Movement

  • Inclusive ads and self-esteem projects challenge beauty norms.
  • ✨ Impact: Sales doubled ($2B → $4B) + 30× earned media ROI.
