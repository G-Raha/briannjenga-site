Website SEO Audits & Competitor Analysis

Before scaling content, fix the foundation. My audits surface indexation problems, thin content, redirect chains, page‑speed bottlenecks, metadata gaps, and weak internal links. I benchmark against direct and search competitors to pinpoint the opportunities you can win now. Deliverables include a prioritized roadmap (quick wins → strategic initiatives), issue explanations, and implementation notes for writers and devs. You’ll know exactly what to fix, what to build, and how to measure impact in GA‑4 and Looker Studio.
